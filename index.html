TBMG COUNTRY — CLASSIFIED LORE DATABASE

1. General Overview of TBMG Country

  • TBMG Country is defined by extreme chaos, radical freedom, and harsh rules. No one actually knows what "TBMG" stands for.
  • The country is split in two. The main city is a lawless disaster area with explosions every day. The countryside is very peaceful and safe.
  • Constant explosions changed the environment, creating glowing plants and adapted animals like "Cinder Foxes". Eventually the nature died and the land became a barren wasteland.

2. Leadership and Government

  • The government is completely corrupt. A small elite group passes power down to their families. A president is only allowed to rule for 50 years.
  • President Tim rules through paranoia and erratic impulses but brought back radical freedoms, like using Photoshop and letting people gather in groups.
  • President Michael was incredibly brutal. It was illegal to speak badly about the government, and punishments included draining people's joyful memories.
  • The true power belongs to the "Dark Leader" or "The Vampire." He never takes an oath, his age makes no sense, and he never raises his voice. People write laws just to avoid upsetting him.

3. Law Enforcement and the Police

  • The Main City Police Department (MCPD) operates like a criminal syndicate according to the FBI.
  • MCPD officers shoot people instantly instead of arresting them. Just bumping into an officer gets you shot.
  • Their ranking system is entirely based on kill count. The MCPD kills about 2 million people every single day.
  • Elite Combine Protection Units (ECPU) outrank all regular officers. They wear opaque helmets, give no warnings, and never hesitate to shoot.

4. Bizarre Laws and Culture

  • Jaywalking is a terrible idea. The government encourages drivers to run over jaywalkers on purpose.
  • Traffic lights are just decorations. Nobody stops for them, but magically, there are zero crashes.
  • It is secretly illegal to shoot a bullet inside a church. Doing this gives you a 736,000-star wanted level.
  • Citizens are allowed to print their own money. But if they get caught doing it, they instantly get a seven-star wanted level.
  • The country hosts absurd events like the "Rubber Ducky Battle" and "Pink Day" where 45% of the population buys mandatory pink paint.
  • A government survival guide tells citizens they must carry 4 Billion Mountain Dew bottles just to look cool.

5. Multiverse Conquest and Technology

  • TBMG Country claims the legal right to invade and destroy any universe based on the "Universal Collapse Treaty 9982-A".
  • The Combine Empire made TBMG its primary force for conquering other universes.
  • Unable to manufacture bullets, TBMG invented weapons firing "Combine Energy Balls". Normal bullets are obsolete.
  • TBMG formed "TBMG NATO", an internet-driven coalition that mocks global power structures.
